The problem

Sand never
stops moving.

Wind carries sand across open desert. Left unchecked, it creeps onto the things we depend on and slowly turns living land into desert.

🌱

Buries vegetation

Drifting sand covers plants and reduces the greenery that holds soil in place.

🛣️

Damages roads & buildings

Sand encroaches on infrastructure, making it costly to keep clear.

🏜️

Leads to desertification

As plants disappear, there is less to stop the wind — and the cycle speeds up.

Our solution

Little barriers make a
big difference.

DuneLock uses interlocking, angled panels that break the wind's flow, slow the sand and create protected pockets where plants can grow. Drag the slider to compare.

With DuneLock

🔁 Movable & adjustable

How it works

Three steps to a
protected landscape.

1

Wind moves sand

Wind pushes sand grains across the open surface towards the barrier.

2

Angled panels break the flow

The slanted, meshed panels disturb the airflow and slow the sand down.

3

Sand settles, plants grow

Sand drops out near the panels, leaving a sheltered area for plants.
